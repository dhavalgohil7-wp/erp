-------
Phase 1 establishes the foundation of the entire School ERP system. Every
subsequent phase depends on the entities, relationships, and infrastructure
built here. Nothing should be skipped. Nothing should be rushed.

    - Theme / branding

1.5 Class & Section Management

1.6 Subject Management
    - Subjects (theory, practical, elective)
